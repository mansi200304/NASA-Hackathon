- No newer NISAR GUNW products exist for Willcox (128 D) or Eloy (077 A) than those already processed; the record ends Oct 21 2026.
- NISAR's own hydrostatic and wet troposphere screens are now subtracted (src/corrections.py; ground heights from the open Copernicus 30 m DEM, since the GUNW has no per-pixel DEM). The solid-earth-tide screen is nearly uniform over each box and changes nothing after referencing, so it is not applied.
- Sign tested, not assumed (src/nisar_noise_test.py, 25 Willcox pairs, same pixels and reference): subtracting hydrostatic+wet cuts pair-to-pair jump scatter 23.3 to 16.9 mm and residual 17.8 to 13.5 mm; the opposite sign makes it 33.5 and 26 mm. So subtracting is the physical sign.
- Willcox NISAR LOS rate: -3.44 +/- 1.23 before, -2.82 +/- 0.93 cm/yr after. Still not stable: -1.1 cm/yr if the series stops at Aug 22; lag-1 correlation -0.47 shows single bad dates remain (Sep 15 2026 is the worst). Not a reliable rate.
- Eloy 077 A (same reference as before, 3.9 months): residual 9.2 to 6.0 mm; rate -3.92 +/- 3.00 before, -4.76 +/- 1.94 cm/yr after. Short record; Sentinel-1 gives about -1.3 at Eloy-Picacho, so this is not used as a result.
- Sentinel-1 results are unchanged. Exports rebuilt (timeseries_*.json, rate_nisar_*.png).
- NISAR vs Sentinel-1 at Willcox, spatial (src/compare_nisar_s1.py, docs/nisar_vs_sentinel1_willcox.png, data/processed/nisar_vs_sentinel1.json). Both descending (NISAR 128 D, Sentinel-1 129 D), 300 m blocks, each map shifted by its own median:
  - NISAR 1-year per-pixel rate vs Sentinel-1 10-year rate, 12,850 blocks: r = 0.581 (0.60 without the Sep 15 date; 0.48 using only the two end dates). Where Sentinel-1 shows sinking (<= -1 cm/yr, 1404 blocks) NISAR shows -3.87 cm/yr relative to the rest, Sentinel-1 -2.27: same place, NISAR larger (short noisy record or recent acceleration; not resolved).
  - Playa circle: NISAR net change over 0.99 yr = -1.74 cm (a bad middle date cancels in the chain), Sentinel-1 10-year trend -1.68 cm/yr and last-3-years -2.74 cm/yr. Consistent within the noise (one date is about 1 to 1.5 cm); not a precise validation.
  - Six-month overlap change (Oct 2025 to Apr 2026): NO agreement (r = -0.172). The signal in six months (about 1 cm) is below the noise, and Sentinel-1 has only two dates in that window (nearest Oct 4 and Apr 14, 22 days off NISAR's start). Reported as a limit.
- When can NISAR confirm a rate? (src/nisar_when.py, data/processed/nisar_confirm.json). Model: independent error on every acquisition date (lag-1 correlation of chain increments measured -0.47 at Willcox and -0.51 at Eloy; the model predicts -0.5). Averaging pixels does not help because a date error is common to the whole scene. Willcox per-date error calibrated to 14.8 mm so the model reproduces the measured OLS standard error (0.93 cm/yr); Eloy 6.7 mm from only 10 dates (weak). Years of continuous 12-day data for a 90% chance of reaching 3 sigma: Willcox basin (S1 rate 2.7 cm/yr) 1.27 yr, about Feb 2027 if there are no gaps; the simulated chance at our current 0.99 yr is only 50%, which matches the unstable rate we saw. Eloy (1.3 cm/yr) 1.21 yr from the Jun 19 2026 start, about Sep 2027. Generic: 1 cm/yr needs 1.5 to 2.5 yr, 0.5 cm/yr needs 2.3 to 4 yr. Covers random per-date error only; reference drift, seasonal motion and LOS-vs-vertical are not included. north_willcox, south and east entries in the file borrow Willcox noise because NISAR was not processed there: do NOT quote them on the site.

## Nov 14 2026, night: ADWR validation built

- ADWR's own published InSAR data downloaded and compared with ours: official subsidence polygons (May 2026), a Sentinel-1 deformation raster (2017 to 2026, same satellite) and a RADARSAT-2 raster (2010 to 2026, independent sensor). Full results and caveats: `docs/adwr_validation.md`.
- ADWR does not document the raster units. Inferred as metres from a regression against our data (slope 0.0093, correlation 0.98).
- Southeast: our medians within 0.01 to 0.11 cm/yr of ADWR's Sentinel-1 and correlation 0.98 to 0.99; against independent RADARSAT-2 correlation 0.94 to 0.98 (medians 0.07 to 0.45 cm/yr apart).
- Eloy (Picacho): weak. Pixel correlation 0.59 (Sentinel-1) and 0.18 (RADARSAT-2). NOT validated; the signal (1 to 2 cm/yr) is near the noise.
- All five study areas' sinking pixels fall 100% inside one matching official ADWR feature (Picacho; Kansas Settlement and Fort Grant Rd; Fort Grant Rd; Elfrida; Bowie-San Simon). Area names now use ADWR's names; the earlier "Elfrida?" guess is confirmed.
- Measured reference offset: ADWR's Sentinel-1 is 0.2 to 0.4 cm/yr more negative than ours over areas with little motion (reference choice). Now part of the published uncertainty: willcox -2.73 +/- 0.38, north -4.89 +/- 0.57, south -3.12 +/- 0.49, east -2.58 +/- 0.57, eloy -1.33 +/- 0.36 cm/yr.
- ADWR's Sentinel-1 raster has no data within 3 km of the four excluded mine points; no ADWR feature there.
- New contract fields in `timeseries_<slug>.json`: `adwr_rate_cm_yr`, `adwr_rs2_rate_cm_yr`, `adwr_feature`, `adwr_comparison`, `adwr_note`. New layer file `adwr_subsidence.geojson` (the existing ADWR layer picks it up).
