# AI assistance disclosure

Per NASA Space Apps rules, this project discloses its use of AI ("Super Intelligence") tools.

An AI coding assistant was used to help with:
- Structuring the project brief and documentation.
- Pair-programming the web map front end (`web/`): the study-area loader, the
  time-series charts and hover interaction, the honest "pending" and
  "preliminary" data states, and layout/styling.
- Drafting plain-language explainer and limits text.

All measured values come from the processing pipeline in `src/` run by the team
on the real NISAR and Sentinel-1 data. No rates, dates or displacements were
generated by an AI tool. Team members reviewed and ran all code, and made the
scientific and design decisions.

No NASA branding or mission identifiers were generated by AI tools.
